01 · The Product

One card. Two ways to work.

Every ROBOWA review card ships with both NFC and QR built in, so it works on any phone — modern Android and iPhone tap it directly, everything else scans the code. Waterproofed, sized for any counter, ready in a day.

02 · How it works

From counter to review page in under 3 seconds.

STEP 01

Customer taps or scans

The card sits at the counter. A satisfied customer holds their phone near it, or scans the printed QR code — whichever they're comfortable with.

STEP 02

Instant redirect

Their phone opens straight to your shop's Google review pop-up — pre-loaded, no search, no typing your business name.

STEP 03

Review, posted

They write a few words and submit — done while the experience is still fresh, right there at your counter.
